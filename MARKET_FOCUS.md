# Where We're Starting (And Why)

> A quick read for founders, operators, and potential partners

---

## We're Not Chasing the Whole TAM

The "agentic AI" market is massive. Everyone's building:
- Voice agents (sales, support)
- AI SDRs
- AI bookkeepers
- AI everything

**We're not trying to win all of it.** That's a recipe for building something generic that fits nobody.

---

## Our Starting Point: Backend Operations

We're starting where most business owners have the most pain and least help:

### The stuff nobody wants to do
- **Admin workflows** - approvals, routing, status updates
- **Data operations** - syncing systems, formatting, validation
- **Basic infra** - deployments, monitoring alerts, config management

### Why this?
1. **Unsexy = underserved.** Voice agents and AI SDRs are crowded. Backend ops isn't.
2. **Measurable ROI.** Hours saved, errors reduced, systems synced.
3. **Builds trust.** When your backend agent works reliably, you trust us with more.

## The Platform Play (Eventually)

Here's what we're actually building toward:

```
Phase 1 (Now):     Backend ops workflows for specific verticals
                              ↓
Phase 2:           Add memory - agents remember context across runs
                              ↓
Phase 3:           Learning layer - agents suggest optimizations
                              ↓
Phase 4:           True autonomy - agents manage operational domains
```

**The endgame is a platform where any operator can build supervised AI agents for their exact workflow.** But we're not shipping vaporware. We're starting with workflows that work.

## Why Supervised Autonomy?

Most agent platforms promise full autonomy. We think that's wrong.

**Full autonomy fails because:**
- AI hallucinates on edge cases
- Business context changes faster than models learn
- One bad autonomous decision = broken trust forever

**Supervised autonomy works because:**
- Human approves high-stakes decisions
- AI handles the 80% grunt work
- Trust is earned, then expanded

See [WHY_SUPERVISED_AUTONOMY.md](WHY_SUPERVISED_AUTONOMY.md) for the technical deep dive.
